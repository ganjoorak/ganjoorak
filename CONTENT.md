## Poems, audio, and third-party mirrors

Publishing this repository does **not** relicense poem text, audio, or other third-party material that the app mirrors or stores. That includes contemporary translations and poems from Echolalia (the Echolalia code path and `data/echolalia/`) and poem files under `public/poems/` and `poems/`. Those works stay with whoever holds their rights.

Redistributors, and anyone who runs this app as a network service, need their own right to serve that material. The AGPL on the code is not that right.

Recitation audio is not in the git tree. `public/audios/` is listed in `.gitignore` and is not shipped with the repository.
